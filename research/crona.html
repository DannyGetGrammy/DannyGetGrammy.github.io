Cross-Modal Navigation with Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

A collaboration framework for visual–acoustic navigation with modality-specialized agents and multi-agent reinforcement learning.

Multi-Agent RLEmbodied NavigationMultimodal Fusion

Overview

CRONA (Cross-Modal Navigation) investigates whether teams of modality-specialized agents can navigate more effectively than a single monolithic policy. Individual agents operate with complementary visual or acoustic observations, while centralized training can draw on broader information to improve coordination.

The work studies how agent specialization, auxiliary beliefs, and centralized critics affect navigation in visually and acoustically rich indoor environments.

My contributions

  • Implemented and evaluated adaptive multimodal baselines, including learned gating and cross-attention fusion.
  • Designed controlled comparisons between single-agent and specialized multi-agent policies, including matched training budgets.
  • Ran ablation, navigation behavior, generalization, and scalability experiments using Habitat and SoundSpaces.
  • Contributed to experiment analysis and manuscript preparation as part of the research team.

Research questions

  • When should distinct audio and visual agents collaborate rather than share one multimodal policy?
  • How robust is modality specialization across new environments and target configurations?
  • What changes as agent populations and policy capacity increase?
